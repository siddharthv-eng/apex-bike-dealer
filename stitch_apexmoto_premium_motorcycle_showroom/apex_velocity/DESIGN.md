## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the adrenaline, technical precision, and bespoke luxury of elite motorsport engineering. Tailored for discerning motorcycle collectors, high-performance riders, and track enthusiasts, the platform evokes raw speed, surgical control, and uncompromising prestige.

The visual direction converges High-Contrast Dark Luxury with High-Performance Motorsport Brutalism. Deep void-black canvases act as dynamic wind tunnels, framed by architectural carbon surfaces, brushed titanium wireframes, and searing crimson telemetry pulses. Visual elements prioritize sharp aerodynamic contours, telemetry readouts, and tactile engineered controls over generic soft interfaces. Every interaction mimics an ignition sequence: crisp, instant, tactile, and engineered for maximum velocity.

## Colors
The palette captures trackside telemetry under floodlights:

- **Primary (`#ff1a35`) & Primary Dark (`#e0061e`)**: High-octane crimson race red used strictly for focal performance states, active telemetry spikes, primary calls-to-action, rev limits, and critical indicators. Never dilute across non-actionable large backgrounds.
- **Titanium & Structural Steels (`#404050`, `#2e2e38`)**: Machine-brushed titanium and metallic grey boundaries. Used for hairline borders, technical spec grids, crosshairs, and inactive control thresholds.
- **Deep Void & Carbon Stack (`#0a0a0c`, `#141418`, `#1c1c22`)**: Pitch black (`#0a0a0c`) forms the canvas substrate. Carbon Tier 1 (`#141418`) and Carbon Tier 2 (`#1c1c22`) represent structural panels, dynamic chassis cards, and telemetry trays.
- **Stark White (`#ffffff`) & Ghost Platinum (`#a1a1aa`, `#71717a`)**: Primary typographic readouts employ pure white for uncompromised contrast against dark carbon plates, while secondary specs and units use dampened cool-spectrum platinums.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm merges the aggressive, expansive architecture of `Syne` with the clinical, unyielding utility of `Inter` and the instrument-cluster mechanics of `JetBrains Mono`.

- **Headlines (`Syne`)**: Used for vehicle names, aerodynamic taglines, and marquee section anchors. Always set tight with intentional tracking compression at massive scale to produce an intimidating visual profile.
- **Body (`Inter`)**: Neutral, neutral-density Swiss geometric styling ensures zero legibility degradation across engineering spec breakdowns, chassis descriptions, and long-form narrative.
- **Technical Metrics & Badges (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict uppercase tracking for displacement (`1,103 CC`), horsepower (`214 HP`), torque curves, rev timers, VIN verifications, and HUD counters.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates on a 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (`1280px+`), collapsing to 6 columns on tablet (`768px-1279px`), and 4 columns on mobile (`<768px`). 

Structural philosophy balances high-density engineering data clusters with vast, cinematographic visual runways for vehicle displays. Section breaks mimic wind-tunnel air corridors: expansive horizontal bands anchored with razor-thin titanium dividing lines. Content density increases within technical readouts, utilizing strict 8px/16px baseline modules for alignment, ensuring zero drift between spec values and corresponding telemetry units.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through mechanical surface layering and luminous chromatic bleed rather than diffuse drop shadows:

1. **Substrate (Base)**: Pitch Black (`#0a0a0c`).
2. **Structural Deck (Level 1)**: Carbon Tier 1 (`#141418`) bordered with 1px brushed titanium hairline (`#2e2e38`).
3. **Elevated Monocoque (Level 2)**: Carbon Tier 2 (`#1c1c22`) layered atop lower panels with 1px active titanium borders (`#404050`).
4. **Active Race Glow**: Critical elements, active buttons, and redline warnings deploy a focused, directional crimson luminescence: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 26, 53, 0.45), 0 0 8px 0 rgba(255, 26, 53, 0.25)`.
5. **Glass Telemetry Overlays**: Semi-opaque black matrices (`rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.85)`) coupled with a sharp 16px backdrop blur, framed with high-precision brushed metallic edges.

## Shapes
In alignment with raw carbon-fiber aero fairings and precision-milled billet aluminum components, the system utilizes a radical sharp geometry (`0px` border-radius). 

No rounded corners exist anywhere in the structural hierarchy. UI surfaces feature 90-degree industrial intersections, chamfered corner cuts (via 45-degree angle clip paths), or beveled precision notches on high-priority badges and HUD widgets. This enforces a no-compromise race posture across every element.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Race Ignition (CTA)**: Solid crimson red (`#ff1a35`), stark white text (`#ffffff`), uppercase `label-md`. Zero radius. Hover triggers an instant transition to `#e0061e` with ambient redline glow and a `1px` white outer flash.
- **Secondary Billet**: Carbon Tier 2 surface (`#1c1c22`), titanium outline (`#404050`), stark white text. Hover swaps border to `#ff1a35` with text shifting to pure white.
- **Ghost Telemetry**: Transparent background, 1px titanium border (`#2e2e38`), monospaced text. Hover triggers a subtle white hair-trigger fill.

### Cards & Chassis Panels
Structured panels framed by `1px` solid `#2e2e38`. On hover, the top border shifts to pure crimson red (`#ff1a35`), while the background subtly deepens into Carbon Tier 2 (`#1c1c22`). Includes technical coordinate tags or chassis numbers in `label-sm` placed in the top-right corner.

### Chips & Badges
Rectangular technical tags. Background: `#141418` with 1px `#404050` border. Monospaced uppercase text (`JetBrains Mono`). Dynamic statuses (e.g., "TRACK ONLY", "SUPERCHARGED", "RESERVED") feature a 6px solid `#ff1a35` indicator dot at the leading edge.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
High-contrast tactical fields. Background `#141418`, border 1px `#2e2e38`, typography stark white. Focus activates a crisp 1px `#ff1a35` border and faint crimson ambient wash, removing default browser outlines entirely. Labels rest strictly above in uppercase `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
Custom angular selectors. Checkboxes are pure squares with a 1px `#404050` border, filling with `#ff1a35` and displaying a sharp diagonal check when active. Radios are sharp diamond shapes that illuminate with a central crimson core when toggled.

### Telemetry HUD Gauges (Specialty Component)
Engine spec modules displaying RPM, power-to-weight, and lean angles. Built with horizontal segmented bar meters using `#2e2e38` for inactive segments and `#ff1a35` for active peak zones, accompanied by `metric-display` numeric readouts.
